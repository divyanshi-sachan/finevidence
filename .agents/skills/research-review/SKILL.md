# Research review

Use when a research decision depends on papers, benchmark documentation, or dataset provenance.

1. Prefer primary sources: official dataset documentation, paper PDFs, code repositories, and benchmark instructions.
2. Record the source URL, access date, and the narrow claim it supports. Quote sparingly and distinguish source facts from project hypotheses.
3. Note dataset-version differences, unreproduced claims, and unresolved licensing or evaluation details.
4. Translate findings into a scoped decision, comparison to test, or open question; do not turn literature claims into local results.
5. Add durable observations to `docs/research-notes.md` and link experiments only after they run.

Do not present a paper's benchmark result as FinEvidence performance or infer permitted use from the dataset name alone.
